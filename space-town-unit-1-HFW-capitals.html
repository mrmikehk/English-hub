How to Play Capital Crater Whack

Space Town Unit 1: Capital Crater Whack is designed for Primary 1 classroom smartboards, tablets, and desktop browsers. Friendly one-eyed and two-eyed space aliens pop out of six moon craters carrying high-frequency sight words on bright white belly badges.

  • Tap Capitalized Words (+10 points): When an alien appears with a word beginning with an uppercase letter (such as My, Is, Am, Five, Six, Old, This, A, Red, Blue) or the pronoun I, tap the alien to score points and hear the word spoken aloud.
  • Leave Lowercase Words Alone (-5 points): If the word starts with a lowercase letter (such as my, is, am, five, six, old, this, a, red, blue), do not whack the alien!

Classroom Smartboard Tips

Invite two or three students to the interactive whiteboard at a time, or let the whole class call out “Whack!” or “Wait!” before a selected student taps the screen. Use the timer dropdown in the top bar to switch between 45-second, 60-second, and 90-second rounds.

English Learning Games

Interactive web applications designed to support primary English language acquisition.

Welcome to Mr. Mike's collection of interactive ESL activities. Designed specifically for primary students, teachers, and parents in Hong Kong, these educational games help early learners develop foundational skills—from sight word recognition and phonics to sentence building and core vocabulary.

Curriculum Alignment: All activities on this platform are specifically tailored to reinforce the Read Write Inc. (RWI) Phonics program and Hong Kong's Space Town Literacy Curriculum.

About the Curriculum Alignment

As a Native English Teacher (NET) in Hong Kong, I develop these interactive web games to directly support the local primary school English syllabus. Whether you are a teacher looking for classroom activities or a parent helping your child practice at home, these resources are built to reinforce key learning objectives from Primary 1 through Key Stage 2.

Space Town Literacy Programme

These games follow the unit-by-unit progression used in Hong Kong primary schools. From early high-frequency sight words to grammar structures like countable and uncountable foods, these activities help students practice targeted vocabulary and sentence building in a fun, engaging environment.

Read Write Inc. (RWI) Phonics

A strong foundation in phonics is essential for early readers. The activities aligned with the RWI program support students in mastering Speed Sounds, blending, and segmenting. These exercises build reading fluency from the initial Ditty level all the way through the more advanced book bands.
